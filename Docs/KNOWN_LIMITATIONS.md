# Known limitations (V1)

- **Illustrative only.** No location, route, gate, restroom, concession or seat view is verified. The seat is an example ticket (Section 111, Row 10, Seat 8), not real ticket data.
- **Example-game clocks are approximate.** The times (Plaza gates 10:00 AM, club gates 11:00, inner gates 11:30, flares 11:50 / 12:00 / 12:15, anthem 12:50, kickoff 1:00 PM) follow a typical 1:00 PM Linc pattern for Sunday, October 18, 2026 (Panthers at Eagles). They are marked Approximate and are not confirmed operations for that game until stadium operations publishes the week's guide.
- **The ticket is the GIF, retyped.** `Tools/Pipeline/prepare_ticket.py` sets the example seat (Section 111, Row 10, Seat 8) and the header "Eagles vs. Carolina Panthers / Sun, October 18 · 1:00 PM" on every frame. The rest of the GIF is unchanged: "South Gate / LOWER LEVEL", "Adult", the barcode and the helmets.
- **Online only.** Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles stream live and can't be cached or used offline. Without a network, the journey UI still works but the stadium surroundings are missing. The recorded video is the offline fallback.
- **Stand-in stadium.** The exterior is the Google tiles' own stadium mesh. Interiors are simple neutral proxies until the CGTrader model is licensed and ingested (`Docs/ASSET_INGESTION.md`).
- **Keyboard focus navigation (Tab) isn't supported.** Keyboard users use the shortcuts shown on each button. On a controller, the settings toggles aren't reachable; use keys 1–4 or the mouse.
- **No inactivity reset** (it's Tier B).
- **MetaHuman crowd is blocked (session 4).** City Sample Vehicles and Free Crowd Cheering Sounds are in `Content/`. The MetaHuman Crowd Sample is enabled as an engine plugin (`MetaHumanCrowdContent` in the .uproject), but the engine's MetaHumanCharacter plugin has no `Content/Optional` folder (skin microtiling textures, grooms), so its characters load headless and grey (33 `LoadErrors` at startup). Install that optional MetaHuman content for UE 5.8 before the crowd pass. The City Sample pack also asks for `r.VirtualTextures=True` (not set; the cars render without it, the MetaHuman `_VT` skin materials may not).
- **Low crowd only, no video boards.** The six line and security people are primitive stand-ins (the nine plaza people are hidden: they read as bollards). The bowl crowd is a thin band of painted cards on the first four rows of the sidelines and north end: it reads as crowd from the aerial and the seat, not up close, and nobody moves. No jerseys (the `Assets/Clothing Textures` jerseys are presentation sheets, not UV albedo maps). Crowd is Low (default) or Off; Calm Mode hides it.
- **The lot's cars are hidden, not streamed out.** Inside the stadium they stop rendering, but their meshes stay in memory (one level, no streaming). The lot is the model's own asphalt apron; the Google tiles' real lots around it keep their photographed cars.
- **Voiceover is ElevenLabs text-to-speech** (voice Sarah, `EXAVITQu4vr4xnSDxMaL`; settings in `VO_MANIFEST.md`), not a recorded human voice. All 23 lines are imported. Voice is Off by default.
- **No karaoke highlight.** The optional word highlight while the voice reads isn't built; it needs word timings from the final recordings.
- **Hero props are built from the brief's description, not the photos.** The popcorn cart and sensory trailer follow the description (colours, awning, lettering); the reference photos weren't in the project. The trailer's mascot art isn't in the project, so the trailer has lettering only. The cart header carries the Wawa and EAF logos from `Assets/Logos` (sponsor marks; LEDGER A5 asks for case-by-case review).
- **Gate proxies are generic.** The three gates (Pepsi Plaza, club, inner) are simple sliding gates at illustrative spots, not the real gate designs or locations.
- **The eagle is a geometry cache,** a 2 s flap loop baked from the glTF (`Tools/Pipeline/prepare_eagle.py`), scaled to a 2.8 m wingspan so it reads from the seats. It flies one straight pass and only in the anthem preview.
- **Finale logos.** The Eagles head (`Assets/Logos`) is a decal at midfield, as on the real field; from the seat (row 10, 5 m up) it reads as a flat sliver. The finale card carries the Eagles Autism Foundation wordmark (`Assets/Logos` has no Eagles-only wordmark).
- **Soft previews only.** The loud-sounds sample is a synthesized, muffled placeholder; the flares and bright-lights previews are an exposure pulse on the camera, not modelled flares or stadium lights; the anthem preview is a stand-in eagle and simple glowing sparks. None is a recording of the real stadium.
- **Placeholder sound.** The plaza murmur and the preview sample are synthesized (`Tools/Audio/make_lff_audio.py`). The seat cheer bed is a 14 s loop cut from the steady middle of the Free Crowd Cheering Sounds "Ambience" take (`Tools/Audio/make_cheer_bed.py`); the pack's cheering takes swell by 9-21 LU, so they aren't used.
- **Stand-ins, not places.** The props at each stop (cars, gates, popcorn cart, sensory trailer, booth, portal, staff) are simple shapes placed for illustration. Their positions aren't the real Popcorn for the People stand, Sensory Activation Vehicle, Guest Services locations or sensory room.
- **Shared cameras.** The inner-gates and flares steps share `CT_InnerGate`; loud sounds, kickoff and the finale share `CT_SeatFieldView`; bright lights and the anthem use the same seat looking up. The concourse steps are all on the plaza-level concourse, and the sensory room is shown from the concourse outside a stand-in door (no interior).
- **The concourse walls have no collision,** so the camera's wall check can't see them. Moves into and out of the concourse are authored as fades.
- **Unsigned builds.** Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper warn on first launch. See the release notes.
- **Win64 is still uncompiled.** The C++ builds with 0 warnings on the Mac (LEDGER §9); MSVC hasn't seen it.
- **English only.** The text uses `LOCTEXT` or Data Table FText, so it's ready to localize later.
