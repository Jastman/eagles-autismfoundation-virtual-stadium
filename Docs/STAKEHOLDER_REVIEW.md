# Stakeholder Review Questions

## For Autistic Users and Accessibility Specialists

### (1) Fine for demo prototype
- Does the 3–4.5 second camera flight feel too fast, too slow, or disorienting?
- Is the text size readable on a large office display from 1–2 meters away?
- Are the button labels and focus order intuitive with a keyboard or controller?

### (2) Needs validation by users and specialists
- Does Calm Mode (instant transitions, muted colors, longer timeouts) feel helpful to some people, or does it feel patronizing?
- Is the inactivity prompt ("Are you still here?") gentle enough, or does a 5-minute timeout feel too long or too short?
- Would sound-on-by-default be better for hearing-focused users, or should it stay off-by-default?
- Do the disclaimer badges feel necessary, or do they create cognitive clutter?
- Should the tour auto-advance to the next step on any input, or should it require an explicit "Next" button press?

### (3) Cannot be represented without verified data
- N/A (all walkthrough content is labeled Illustrative or Approximate).

## For Eagles Autism Foundation and Stadium Ops

### (1) Fine for demo prototype
- Does the venue branding (Lincoln Financial Field name, neutral placeholder art) meet approval?
- Is the overall tone helpful and non-patronizing?

### (2) Needs validation by stadium ops and security
- Is the "Gate Entry / Security" description accurate enough for a preview, or does it misrepresent bag-check procedures?
- Can you verify or revise the Arrival, Concourse, and Seating locations once the 3D model is placed?
- Do you have a sensory room location and hours we can reference, or should we keep it as a placeholder ("ask staff")?
- Are there any restricted areas or safety hazards we should warn about in the preview?

### (3) Cannot be represented without verified data
- Interior gate/security layouts (no verified scan data; using placeholder).
- Restroom locations and capacity (data not provided).
- Concessions locations and menus (vary by season; using placeholder).
- Sensory room or quiet-space location, capacity, access rules (data not provided).
- Exact seating map and ADA-accessible seat inventory (external to this prototype; link to official Eagles ticketing).

## Open questions for lead and stakeholders

1. **Calm Mode default for kiosk:** Should the first user to touch the screen see Calm Mode on or off? Recommend off (full demo experience), but stakeholders may prefer on (safer default).

2. **Noise previews in v1:** Should we include stadium-crowd audio with a warning button, or defer to v2? Recommend defer; text descriptions are sufficient.

3. **Sensory room verification:** Does the Eagles Autism Foundation have verified data on any sensory or quiet spaces? If not, we keep the placeholder and recommend "ask staff."

4. **Branding sign-off:** Have legal and the Eagles Autism Foundation approved the use of "Lincoln Financial Field" and any Eagles marks in the copy?

5. **Hardware and network:** Will the demo kiosk be online or offline? Does the display support 1080p, 1440p, or 4K? This affects text sizing rules.
